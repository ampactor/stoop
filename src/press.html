STOOP PRESS one sheet → eight pages → one cut → your first issue. no software after this point.

PRINT

letter · landscape · scale 100% · margins none. click any panel text to edit it before printing; better yet, print the headers and fill the rest by hand. pens beat keyboards here.

honesty clause: printers and folding hands vary. print ONE test sheet, fold it, check the pages read 1→8. wrong order? swap layout, reprint. then print the run.

two people is a scene. the back cover has a line for the issue's permanent address; the paper and the page are the same issue, and each one points at the other.

FOLD (five steps)

  1. Fold in half the long way. Crease hard. Unfold.
  2. Fold in half the short way, then each end back to the middle. Unfold everything: eight panels.
  3. Fold the long way again. Cut along the crease from the folded edge, across the middle two panels only. Stop at the quarter creases.
  4. Unfold. Fold the long way, slit on top. Hold both ends, push toward the middle: the slit opens; four wings make a plus sign.
  5. Collapse the wings around one panel and crease it into a book. The №01 cover should land on front. Read it once, then hand it to somebody.

this layout prints the top row upside-down on purpose. that's not a bug; that's the fold. staples optional, one in the spine if you're feeling official.
