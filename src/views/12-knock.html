THE PUBLIC STOOP night bus's door

ANYONE CAN KNOCK.

vouching may be selective; reachability may not. every scene owes the world one visible door, and this is ours. a scene with no door is a clique, and cliques are not scenes under the charter (art. II.4).

HOW YOU'D GET IN

members stake their names on you: two of them, or half the scene, whichever is smaller — one name in a young scene, two once it reaches four. the vouch is listed on your room for as long as you're here; trust is social collateral, not a CAPTCHA. pseudonyms are protected. nobody will ever ask for your documents or your face. nothing synthetic gets membership: in an internet that is majority bot, everyone here is a human somebody could have dinner with. that's the product.

knock: say who you are, what you make, and which member you've met (or come to a show first — friday works).
