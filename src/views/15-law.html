THE LAW five clauses no vote can touch

No advertising

no third party may pay for placement, reach, or attention. ever, in any form, under any name.

No engagement metrics

no follower counts, like tallies, view counts, trending, or ranking by attention. nothing here may be optimized against a measurement of engagement. the system must never learn what a DAU is.

No sale

the co-op, the protocol namespace, and the data cannot be sold, merged into a for-profit, or pledged as collateral. there is no throat to choke and nothing to acquire.

Open everything

all software AGPL. all schemas public. all books open to members. a budget that cannot survive being read does not get adopted.

Credible exit

any member exports their room and graph at any moment. any scene leaves any host and loses nothing. any design that makes leaving costlier than staying is void.

no floor on a scene: two people who publish are a scene, and the roll of who belongs is theirs to keep unpublished (art. I.3) — a roster is a list that gets used.

the economics under it: a scene of two costs nothing but paper; ~25 members × ~$3 dues ≈ $75/mo against single-digit hosting, because there is no feed, no video, no global surface — surplus goes to editor tips, the tool library, the print run, the show. capture requires investors or hostages; dues and forks provide neither. full text: CHARTER.md in the repo.
