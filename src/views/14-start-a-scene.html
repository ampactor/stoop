START A SCENE

the entire onboarding. your group chat deserves a zine.

1NAME THE CREW

2PICK THE BELL

3VOUCH YOUR PEOPLE

two is a scene. there is no minimum here and never will be: you and one other person publishing on a rhythm is the whole thing working. two people ship on a deadline; eight people with no habit miss the first bell. add person three when person three can be handed a back issue.

names on every member, listed on their room: two vouchers, or half the scene, whichever is smaller. founders vouch themselves. paste no contact list; invite by hand. the cap is 150 and then you fork like a good scene.

4TAKE YOUR ADDRESS

every issue gets a permanent link, because a zine you can't hand to somebody isn't doing its job. /stoop/<your scene>/01/ is issue one, /01/sheet is the same issue imposed for a printer, and the scene root is your public door and your shelf. the paper prints the link; the page prints the paper. links inside are relative, so the whole thing moves hosts by being copied.

everyone gets a room (default skin: bone and ink until they touch it). the group chat stays where it is; it becomes the backstage. dues start when there's a bill, not before; a scene of two costs nothing but paper. books open, surplus buys the print run. first issue within one cycle.

nothing here is real until issue #2 ships. that is the founding law of growth.
