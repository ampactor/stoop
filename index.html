HOW TO FOLD (5 Steps)

  1. Fold in half lengthwise (hot dog fold): Crease sharply and unfold.
  2. Fold in half widthwise (hamburger fold): Then fold each outer edge to the center crease to make 8 equal panels. Unfold.
  3. Cut the center slit: Fold in half widthwise again, and cut along the center horizontal crease across the two inner panels only. Stop at the quarter creases.
  4. Pop into a plus sign: Unfold, fold lengthwise with the cut slit on top. Push both outer ends inward so the slit opens into a diamond and then a 4-winged plus sign (+).
  5. Fold into a booklet: Collapse the wings around the front cover. You now have an 8-page pocket zine!
