THE PUBLIC STOOP night bus's door

ANYONE CAN KNOCK.

vouching may be selective; reachability may not. every scene owes the world one visible door, and this is ours. a scene with no door is a clique, and cliques are not scenes under the charter (art. II.3).

HOW YOU'D GET IN

two members stake their names on you. the vouch is listed on your room for as long as you're here; trust is social collateral, not a CAPTCHA. pseudonyms are protected. nobody will ever ask for your documents or your face. nothing synthetic gets membership: in an internet that is majority bot, everyone here is a human somebody could have dinner with. that's the product.

knock: say who you are, what you make, and which member you've met (or come to a show first — friday works).

START A SCENE

the entire onboarding. your group chat deserves a zine.

1NAME THE CREW

2PICK THE BELL

3VOUCH YOUR PEOPLE

two names on every member, listed on their room. paste no contact list; invite by hand. eight is plenty to start; the cap is 150 and then you fork like a good scene.

everyone gets a room (default skin: bone and ink until they touch it). the group chat stays where it is; it becomes the backstage. dues of about $3 a month once it's real, books open, surplus buys the print run. first issue within one cycle.

nothing here is real until issue #2 ships. that is the founding law of growth.

THE LAW five clauses no vote can touch

No advertising

no third party may pay for placement, reach, or attention. ever, in any form, under any name.

No engagement metrics

no follower counts, like tallies, view counts, trending, or ranking by attention. nothing here may be optimized against a measurement of engagement. the system must never learn what a DAU is.

No sale

the co-op, the protocol namespace, and the data cannot be sold, merged into a for-profit, or pledged as collateral. there is no throat to choke and nothing to acquire.

Open everything

all software AGPL. all schemas public. all books open to members. a budget that cannot survive being read does not get adopted.

Credible exit

any member exports their room and graph at any moment. any scene leaves any host and loses nothing. any design that makes leaving costlier than staying is void.

the economics under it: ~25 members × ~$3 dues ≈ $75/mo against single-digit hosting, because there is no feed, no video, no global surface — surplus goes to editor tips, the tool library, the print run, the show. capture requires investors or hostages; dues and forks provide neither. full text: CHARTER.md in the repo.
